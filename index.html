How to play

When you shoot

  • Swipe from the ball up toward the goal. The ball goes where your finger lifts.
  • Swipe fast for a hard shot. Too slow is easy to save. Too wild goes astray.
  • Curve your swipe like a rainbow to bend the ball and fool the keeper.
  • Double-tap the ball to do a fake-out. Some keepers dive the wrong way. Then shoot the other side. One fake per kick.
  • Watch the keeper. He leans before he dives, but he does not always tell the truth.
  • Goals in a row make a combo. At x3 your ball is on fire. A hard shot into a corner is a PERFECT shot.
  • Stars: win a round for 1 star, with one miss or fewer for 2, with no misses for 3.

When you keep goal

  • Tap where you think the ball is going. Tap early to reach the corners.
  • Tap again after you dive to change direction. It is slower than the first dive, so do it early. The other keepers can change their minds too!

World Shootout

  • Pick a country and play the whole cup: a group, then quarter-finals, semi-finals and the final. Every match is penalties. Win matches to collect stickers and the trophy!
